Protopica in DC!

The moment has arrived!

SUBMERGE Project is opening at ARTECHOUSE DC this September, and Protopica will be part of it.

A groundbreaking immersive exhibition where the world’s leading digital artists push beyond the limits of the screen.

🦋 PROTOPICA • Sin Agua No Hay Tiempo
🔮 SUBMERGE Project
📍 ARTECHOUSE DC
🗓️ Open September 11, 2026
🎟️ Ticket link

Presented by ARTECHOUSE, powered by the Render Network.

ABOUT THE EXHIBITION



ARTECHOUSE DC’s next exhibition features fourteen bold worlds and 18 interactive installations crafted with the tools of tomorrow.

What happens when today’s leading digital artists are given the runway to create at the speed of their imagination? Find out at SUBMERGE Project.

Anchored by Beyond the Render, this 45-minute show transforms ARTECHOUSE DC’s floor-to-ceiling canvas into a vivid, 280-degree, 18K digital world. Powered by Render Network’s distributed GPU infrastructure, 13 artist teams—including Emmy winners Emily Yang (pplpleasr) and Maciej Kuciara—bring studio-scale, spatial storytelling to independent digital art like never before.

WHAT TO EXPECT:



A 45-minute program of 14 short, back-to-back works, each at most approximately three minutes, from 17 visionary digital artists. Across the lineup, you’ll dive into underwater realms, discover immersive versions of Emmy Award–winning creations, and step into sleek, futuristic worlds. Unwind to a stained-glass music box that captivated Coachella audiences, and leave inspired by the future of digital art.

18 auxiliary installations extend the exploration of authorship and perception from the immersive canvas into direct, physical exchange.
